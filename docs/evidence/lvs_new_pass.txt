2. WHY THE FOUR EARLIER "DO NOT MATCH" RUNS FAILED - three tool bugs, not layout
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Physical cells were stripped by line, but SPICE wraps long lines with "+".
    Removing X lines matched 884 / 6,380 lines on the two sides and left the two
    netlists inconsistent: layout 16,063 devices vs netlist 15,839/no-fill,
    89,038 vs 89,242 with physical cells kept.
    Fix: join "+" continuation lines first, then drop physical instances.
    Result: 15,588 devices on BOTH sides, and the per-cell-type instance
    inventory is identical - zero cell types differ.

(b) Substrate nets. Layout names one body net per instance pin
    (sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_2_71/VPB); the netlist has one global VPB/VNB.
    Fix: merge VPB->VPWR and VNB->VGND in BOTH circuits (a symmetric relabel,
    no topology change, applied after the hierarchy is built so cell-internal
    bodies move too). Earlier I merged only the netlist, which shortened every
    cell by two nets and produced the false "units differ by 2 nets" reading -
    that reading is withdrawn.

(c) Top-level ports. My verilog->spice converter collapsed vectors into a
    single net, so the top had 18 ports against 97 in the layout, and the four
    continuous assigns
        assign s_axil_bresp[0] = net389; ... assign s_axil_rresp[1] = net392;
    were dropped, leaving four output ports with no net. netgen then reported
    "Top level cell failed pin matching".
    Fix: take the port list from the layout netlist, keep bit-exact names
    (s_axil_araddr[0] ...), apply the four assign aliases, map pins in PDK
    subckt order.

(d) Power pins. write_verilog emitted no PG pins at all (".VGND(" occurs 0 times
    in fixed2.nl.v), so every cell's VGND/VPWR body contact was missing.
    Fix: tie missing VGND/VPWR/VPB/VNB pins to the rails (this is what the
    physical design does), and give every *other* truly unconnected pin a
    unique per-instance name (206 such pins). Naming them per pin type instead
    merged 203 nets and made the net counts differ by exactly 203.
